Romantic Skyline Chill Loop

Slow, easy, scenic. Cruise from Lewis Wharf toward East Boston for the best skyline angle, then pass the Seaport/ICA side without going beyond Legal Harborside.

  1. Depart Lewis Wharf slowly.
  2. Cross toward East Boston / ReelHouse area and drift in neutral where traffic is light.
  3. Angle toward Boston Harbor Hotel / Rowes Wharf for skyline photos.
  4. Glide along Seaport / ICA, turning back before the Legal Harborside boundary.
  5. Return to Lewis Wharf with extra time for a second skyline drift.
No anchoring. It is okay to float in neutral if safe and away from traffic. Do not pass the Hyatt Logan / Legal Harborside line.
Most variety / about 2 hr

Inner Harbor Greatest Hits

Best backup route if the group wants landmarks: Charlestown Navy Yard, USS Constitution area, Zakim Bridge views, East Boston skyline, and the Seaport boundary turn-around.

  1. Lewis Wharf to Charlestown Navy Yard / USS Constitution area.
  2. Nose toward the Zakim Bridge view, but do not go through the Charles River locks.
  3. Loop back through the inner harbor toward East Boston.
  4. Cross to Seaport / ICA and turn back before Legal Harborside.
Landmarks / about 2 hr

History + Architecture Loop

Historic waterfront feel: North End, Coast Guard Base area, Charlestown Navy Yard, USS Constitution, Cassin Young, Zakim Bridge viewpoint, then back through downtown wharves.

  1. Start at Lewis Wharf and hug the North End waterfront.
  2. Pass the Coast Guard Base area as a point of reference only.
  3. Continue toward USS Constitution / Cassin Young.
  4. View Zakim from the harbor side; do not enter the locks.
  5. Return via Boston Harbor Hotel / Rowes Wharf.
